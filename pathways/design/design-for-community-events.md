# Design for Community Events

Help WordPress meetups and WordCamps by designing posters, social media graphics, and other materials they need to promote their events.

- **Reference:** [Design Team Handbook](https://make.wordpress.org/design/handbook/)
- **Connect:** Join [#community-team](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/community-team) on Slack and introduce yourself

## Steps

1. **Find a request.** Check the Design view on the [Good First Issues board](https://github.com/orgs/WordPress/projects/289/views/2) for open design requests. Alternatively, ask your local meetup if they need graphics and have them [file a request](https://github.com/WordPress/Community-Team/issues/new?template=02-design-request.yml).

2. **Claim the work.** Start a thread in the Slack channel listed in the request (usually #community-team), @mentioning the requester, and link back to your GitHub comment. Then comment on the GitHub issue to say you're working on it, and link to that thread. 

3. **Create and iterate.** Work with the requester to understand their needs. Share drafts in your Slack thread for quick feedback. For design-specific questions or reviews, share in #design.

4. **Deliver the files.** Upload final files to the GitHub issue in the formats requested (usually high-res PNG/PDF for print, web-optimized versions for digital), and close the issue.

## Contribution checklist

- Claimed the issue on GitHub
- Started a Slack thread with the requester
- Delivered files in requested formats and resolutions
- Closed the issue with final deliverables

## What happens next

Each completed request earns contributor credit. After a few successful projects, consider joining regular Design team meetings or taking on more complex design challenges. Requesters might come back to you directly for future needs.

## Help

Stuck? Check the [getting help guide](https://make.wordpress.org/handbook/pathways/before-you-begin/#getting-help), then ask in [#community-team](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/C037W5S7X) for process questions or [#design](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/C02S78ZAL) for design help.
