# Write a HelpHub Article or DevHub Tutorial

Write a new article for HelpHub, or a developer tutorial for DevHub. Claim a documentation issue, draft the page, and send it for review.

- **Reference:** [Editing articles](https://make.wordpress.org/docs/handbook/helphub/migrating-articles/)
- **Connect:** Join [#docs](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/docs) on Slack and introduce yourself

## Steps

1. **Pick an issue.** Open the [Documentation Issue Tracker](https://github.com/WordPress/Documentation-Issue-Tracker/issues) and look through the [good first issues](https://github.com/WordPress/Documentation-Issue-Tracker/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good%20first%20issue%22). Choose an open issue that asks for a new page or tutorial. The issue title shows whether the page belongs on [HelpHub](https://wordpress.org/documentation/) or [DevHub](https://developer.wordpress.org/).

2. **Claim the issue.** Comment that you will write the draft. Wait until someone assigns it to you and moves it to In progress.

3. **Write the draft.** Follow the [Documentation Style Guide](https://make.wordpress.org/docs/style-guide/). Say what the reader will do, list what they need, then give numbered steps with one task each. Write in the second person, and keep the page to one topic.

4. **Ask for review.** Post the draft as a comment on the issue stating that the issue is ready for review.

## Contribution checklist

- Claimed an open issue that asks for a new page or tutorial
- Draft follows the Documentation Style Guide
- Draft posted on the issue for review

## What happens next

A Documentation team reviewer reads the draft and may ask for changes. After it is approved, the page is published on the site named in the issue. If you don't hear back, ask in [#docs](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/docs).

When you're ready, pick up another writing issue, or help [review existing documentation fixes](https://make.wordpress.org/handbook/pathways/write/work-on-existing-issues/).

## Help
