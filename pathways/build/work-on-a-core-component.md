# Work on a Core Component

Pick a part of WordPress Core, called a component, and help move its tickets forward in a bug scrub. You'll learn how Core development works without writing code, and maintainers get help confirming and sorting bugs.

- **Reference:** [Core Components](https://make.wordpress.org/core/components/) — each component's maintainers, Slack channel, and tickets
- **Reference:** [Leading Bug Scrubs](https://make.wordpress.org/core/handbook/tutorials/leading-bug-scrubs/) — how a scrub runs
- **Connect:** Join [#core](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/core) on Slack and introduce yourself

## Steps

1. **Log in to Trac.** Trac is Core's ticket tracker. Sign in at [core.trac.wordpress.org](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/) with your WordPress.org account.

2. **Choose a component.** Browse the [components page](https://make.wordpress.org/core/components/) and pick one that matches your interests or experience, such as Media, Editor, or REST API. Follow it by adjusting your [notifications](https://make.wordpress.org/core/notifications/) so you see new activity.

3. **Learn how it works.** Read the component's page and skim its recent tickets in Trac. For larger projects, see the [features page](https://make.wordpress.org/core/features/).

4. **Join a bug scrub.** A bug scrub is a scheduled meeting in Slack where a group works through Trac tickets. Find the current dates in the latest [bug scrub schedule](https://make.wordpress.org/core/tag/bug-scrub/), then join [#core](https://wordpress.slack.com/archives/core) at that time. No experience is required. Some components also run their own scrubs in their own channels.

5. **Work on a ticket.** When the group posts a ticket, help move it forward:
   - Reproduce the bug, or test the attached patch (see [Testing a Patch](https://make.wordpress.org/core/handbook/testing/patch/)).
   - Suggest [keywords](https://make.wordpress.org/core/handbook/contribute/trac/keywords/) that match what you found.

6. **Comment on the ticket.** Post what you tested, your environment, and the result. Short, specific notes help the next person pick it up.

## Contribution checklist

- Followed one component
- Attended a bug scrub in #core
- Reproduced or tested at least one ticket
- Posted a comment on the Trac ticket with your results

## What happens next

Component maintainers and scrub leaders review ticket activity, and tickets move at the pace of the release cycle, so replies can take a few days. If a ticket stalls, ask in #core or ping a component maintainer.

When you're comfortable with how tickets work, try [Fix a Core Good First Bug](https://make.wordpress.org/handbook/pathways/build/fix-a-core-good-first-bug/) to write your first fix.
